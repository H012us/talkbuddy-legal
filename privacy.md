TalkBuddy ("we", "us") is an app for practicing spoken English with an AI conversation partner. It is provided by [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME], [COUNTRY]. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it goes, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

**Short version:**
- We never record or store your voice.
- What you say is turned into text, and that text is sent to our AI provider to create replies and feedback.
- Conversations are deleted after 180 days, or one day after each session if you turn history off.

- TalkBuddy is for people aged 16 and over.

## 2. Your voice

When you tap to talk, your phone's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. **We never receive, record or store your voice.** Depending on your phone and its settings, the speech recognition service of your device's maker (for example Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) may process the audio under its own privacy policy. We receive only the resulting text.

## 4. Advertising

Free sessions are supported by ads from **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

When you watch an ad to unlock a session, we send Google your random account ID and the session's ID so Google can confirm the ad was watched. We do not use your conversations for advertising.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Turn history off**: Settings → "Keep my conversation history". Conversations and feedback are then deleted one day after each session.
- **Delete your account**: Settings → "Delete my account" permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our systems right away.
- **Ads**: change ad consent in the app where offered, and manage your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
- **Access, correction, copies and other rights**: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data-protection authority. ## 7. Children

TalkBuddy is not intended for anyone under 16. The app asks for your birth year and does not create an account for people under 16. If you believe someone under 16 is using TalkBuddy, contact us and we will delete the account.

## 8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to each person's data is restricted on the server so that one user cannot see another's. AI keys are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.
